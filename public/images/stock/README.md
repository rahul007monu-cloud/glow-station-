# Placeholder photos

These are free stock photos used as placeholders until Diksha's own work photos are ready.

To replace them, drop new photos into `public/images/` and update the paths in `src/data/salon.ts`:
- `categories[].photo` (exploded menu cards)
- `story[].photo` (scroll story scenes)
- `gallery[].src` (3D photo ring)

Optional: put a short vertical or landscape video at `public/media/divine.mp4`. The scroll-scrubbed film section appears automatically once that file exists.
