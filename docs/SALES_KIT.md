# Sales kit — salon website + app kaise bechna hai

Ye document salon owner ke saamne baat karne ke liye hai. Technical shabd
istemaal nahi karne — owner ko sirf teen cheezein sunni hain: **time bachega,
booking badhegi, paisa dikhega.**

---

## 1. 30-second pitch (pehli line yahi bolna)

> "Aapka salon Instagram par acha chal raha hai, par jab customer 'salon near
> me' Google par search karta hai to aap nahi dikhte. Main aapko ek website +
> mobile app de raha hoon jisme customer khud slot choose karke booking
> WhatsApp par bhej deta hai — aapko sirf 'yes' likhna hai. Naam, number,
> service, time, total — sab ready message me aata hai. Aur ye app customer ke
> phone me install ho jaata hai, to wo aapko bhoolta nahi."

Phir turant phone nikaalo aur **demo dikhao** — bolna kam, dikhana zyada.

## 3. USP — owner ki bhasha me

Har USP ka format: **problem → solution → faayda**.

### A. Kaam aasaan hone wale (time + jhanjhat bachta hai)

| Problem jo owner rozana jhelta hai | Humara solution | Faayda |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Din bhar "rate kya hai?" wale calls | Website par transparent price list | Time-wasting calls 50%+ kam |
| Booking notebook/memory me, double booking | 3-step booking → WhatsApp par ready message | Double booking khatam, walk-in waiting kam |
| Staff ko har baar samjhana | App me service, price, duration sab fixed | Naya staff pehle din se sahi rate bolta hai |
| Kaun customer kab aaya — record nahi | Admin dashboard + CSV export | Excel me data, mahine ka hisaab 2 minute me |
| Offers batane ke liye har customer ko call | App me offer + countdown, code copy | Ek jagah, sabko dikhta hai |
| "Kaun ad kaam kar raha hai" pata nahi | Har booking me lead source likha aata hai | Paisa sirf chalne wale ad me lagega |

### B. Leads aur sales badhane wale

| Feature | Kaise sale badhti hai |
| --- | --- |
| **Packages with saving maths** (`Aap bachate ho ₹901`) | Customer ₹599 ki service ki jagah ₹1,499 ka membership leta hai → **ticket size 2-3x** |
| **Seats left counter** | "Sirf 8 seats" → faisla aaj hota hai, "baad me" nahi |
| **First-visit 25% off popup** | Naya visitor jaane se pehle number de deta hai |
| **Loyalty card (6 visit = free reward)** | Customer dusre salon nahi jaata → repeat visits |
| **Referral code** | Ek khush customer = 1-2 naye customer, **zero ad kharcha** |
| **WhatsApp 1-tap** | Form bharne ka jhanjhat nahi → 3-4x zyada log contact karte hain |
| **Google par dikhna (SEO + local schema)** | "salon near me" walo se free leads |
| **App install** | Icon phone me rehta hai → customer bhoolta nahi, dobara aata hai |
| **Meta Pixel ready** | Ad platform ko pata chalta hai kaunsa customer book karta hai → wahi jaise log dikhte hain → **lead cost girta hai** |
| **Bridal/Academy ke alag package** | High-ticket (₹25k-₹35k) enquiry bhi aati hai, sirf ₹300 ka haircut nahi |

### C. Bharosa banane wale (trust = zyada price)

- Aapke asli photos, asli logo, asli board ka design — **template jaisa nahi lagta**
- Hygiene, branded products, certified stylist — sab likha hua
- Reviews section + Google review link
- Website secure (https) aur phone par 1 second me khulti hai
- **Offline bhi khulti hai** — network na ho to bhi menu dikhta hai

## 4. Paisa ka hisaab (yahi baat close karti hai)

Ye calculation owner ke saamne likh ke dikhao:

```
Aapka average bill                    ₹600
Website se roz sirf 1 extra booking   ₹600 x 30 = ₹18,000 / mahina
Website ka one-time kharcha           ₹15,000
                                      --------------------------------
Pehle mahine me hi paisa wapas + ₹3,000 profit
```

Aur agar package bikta hai:

```
1 membership (₹1,499) x 10 customer   = ₹14,990 / mahina extra
1 bridal package (₹24,999) x 1        = ₹24,999
```

> Line bolna: **"Agar mahine me 2 booking bhi extra aayi, website free ho gayi.
> Baaki sab profit hai."**

## 6. Objection handling (ratt lo)

**"Mera Instagram to chal raha hai, website ki kya zarurat?"**
> "Instagram par aap tab dikhte ho jab customer aapko follow karta hai. Website par
> tab dikhte ho jab wo 'salon near me' search karta hai — wo customer aapko
> jaanta bhi nahi, phir bhi aata hai. Aur Instagram me booking DM me kho jaati
> hai, yahan ready message aata hai. Dono saath chalenge — website ka link aapke
> bio me lagega."

**"JustDial par hoon, wahan se call aate hain."**
> "JustDial par aapke saath 20 salon dikhte hain aur wo har lead ka paisa leta
> hai. Ye aapka apna hai — ek baar ka kharcha, lead free."

**"Bahut mehnga lag raha hai."**
> "Ek keratin treatment ₹5,000 ka hai na? Mahine me 3 extra customer aaye to
> website ka paisa nikal gaya. Aur ye 3 saal chalegi. Chahe to Starter se shuru
> karo, baad me upgrade kar lenge."

**"Mujhe technical nahi aata, kaun chalayega?"**
> "Kuch nahi karna. Booking WhatsApp par aayegi jaise normal message. Offer
> badalna ho to mujhe WhatsApp karo, 1 ghante me ho jaayega. Aur ek panel hai
> jahan saari booking list dikhti hai — bas PIN daalna hai."

**"Photos nahi hain mere paas."**
> "Aaj hi phone se 10 photo bhej do — chair, mirror, aapka kaam, before-after.
> Main lagaa dunga. Chaho to shoot bhi karwa dunga."

**"Baad me karenge / soch ke batata hoon."**
> "Bilkul. Ek kaam karte hain — main aapke salon ke naam se demo bana ke bhejta
> hoon, aap dekh lo. Aur haan, is mahine ke sirf 2 slot khali hain kyunki har
> project par 3-4 din lagte hain." *(demo bana ke bhejna = 60% close rate)*

**"Kitna time lagega?"**
> "Content mil gaya to 3-5 din. Live link me aapko dikha dunga, phir changes
> karke final karenge."
